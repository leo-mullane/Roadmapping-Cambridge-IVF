Prototype 1 of 5 · Clear costs

What we're proposing

Replace the long prices page with one that answers the question people actually have, which is "what will this cost me?" A short estimator gives a likely total and how it's paid in stages. The packages sit side by side. Every optional add-on carries the HFEA's own rating of the evidence for it, and a short section explains the ways to pay.

Why

Cambridge IVF publishes its prices in full, which many clinics don't, and still 16 of the 22 people who have rated it on the HFEA site say they paid "way above the estimate". The prices page runs to about 8,000 pixels on a phone, repeats the add-on list under nearly every package and gives different PURE criteria from the PURE page. None of the add-ons mention that the HFEA rates time-lapse imaging and PICSI as making no difference to the chance of a baby. An honest total up front should mean fewer people dropping out after an expensive first consultation, more choosing the Duo and Multi-Cycle packages, and a better score on the comparison site most self-funders check.

Prices are those on the live prices page. Criteria and add-on wording are for the clinical team to confirm.

      Add-ons and the evidence

      Add-ons are optional extras on top of IVF. The HFEA, the regulator, rates whether good evidence shows each one improves the chance of having a baby. We show its rating next to everything we offer, and we'll only suggest an add-on when there's a reason it may help you.

      What the colours mean

      • Green Good evidence it helps
      • Amber Studies disagree
      • Grey Not enough good evidence
      • Black Good evidence it makes no difference
      • Red May reduce success, or safety concerns

      Ratings are the HFEA's, for the chance of having a baby for most patients. Some add-ons have other ratings for particular groups. Read the HFEA's guide to add-ons.
