What we're proposing

One place to take the first step, in whichever way suits the person: a short enquiry, a free call with the patient team at a time they choose, a place at an information session, or a full self-referral with their previous results attached. Each ends with a clear "what happens next". A guide sign-up catches the people who aren't ready yet.

Why

There isn't a single form on the site. Self-referral is "by email or post", appointments are by phone, and the information sessions are booked on a separate site with no date shown on the homepage. Most people research fertility treatment in the evening, when the phone lines are closed. Every competitor we looked at takes enquiries on the page, and Bourn Hall, ten miles away, makes "Book a discovery call" its main button. This is the change most likely to bring in more patients, and the one that lets the clinic count them for the first time.

Because enquiries contain health information, the real thing should use a form tool the Trust already approves, with a data protection impact assessment.
