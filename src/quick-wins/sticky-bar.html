What we're proposing

A slim bar at the bottom of the screen on phones with Call, Information session and Enquire, on service, price and package pages.

Why

On a phone the prices page is about 8,000 pixels long and has no call to action above the fold. People make up their minds while reading the long pages, and at that moment the next step is several screens away. A sticky bar is a small template change for the CUH web team.
