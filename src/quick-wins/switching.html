Moving your treatment to us

Moving clinics is more common than people think, and it’s straightforward.

  1. Send us your records: previous cycles, test results and any letters.
  2. Book a review consultation (£220) to agree a plan.
  3. If you have frozen eggs or embryos, we arrange the transfer with your current clinic.
