Quick win 21 of 22 · Look and findability

What we're proposing

Use the empty right-hand side of content pages for a “Next step” panel: price, next information session and an enquiry button.

Why

At desktop width, content pages sit in a 640-pixel column with nothing beside them. That space is where a reader’s eye goes when they’ve decided they’re interested. A sticky panel with the price and the next step puts the call to action beside the content that persuades, without adding any content.
