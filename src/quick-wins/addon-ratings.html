Quick win 2 of 22 · Costs and packages

What we're proposing

Show the HFEA’s traffic-light rating beside every optional add-on the clinic sells, with a line on why it might be suggested.

Why

The prices page lists PICSI, Zymot, MACS, calcium ionophore, endometrial receptivity testing and DNA fragmentation testing without a word about the evidence. The HFEA rates PICSI and time-lapse imaging black (no effect on the chance of a baby for most patients), EmbryoGlue amber and endometrial receptivity testing red. An NHS clinic is the place patients would expect to be told this. It may trim some add-on sales; it also protects the clinic from complaints and gives it something private rivals are reluctant to copy.
