Quick win 16 of 22 · Content clean-up

What we're proposing

Use plain headings on the support information page instead of the NHS “Non-urgent advice” card, and link to the lab page rather than repeating it.

Why

The support information page is about 41,000 characters long and labels every section “Non-urgent advice”, including the glossary and a list of useful websites. On NHS sites that label means something specific about when to seek care. The page also repeats the whole “What happens in the lab?” page. Plain headings and a link would make it shorter and clearer.
