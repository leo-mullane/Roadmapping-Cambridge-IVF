What we're proposing

Turn on GA4 events for phone and email taps, information session bookings and price-list downloads, and send a one-page report each month.

Why

GA4 and Google Tag Manager are already on the site, but there are no forms to measure and we saw no sign of events on phone numbers, email links, the TryBooking link or the PDF price lists. Without them, nobody can say how many people the website sends to the clinic, or whether a change helped. Setting up the events is a few hours in Tag Manager; the report is a template someone fills in monthly.
