Quick win 1 of 22 · Costs and packages

What we're proposing

Agree one set of PURE criteria with the clinical lead and make the prices page and the PURE page say the same thing.

Why

Two different sets of PURE criteria are live today. The prices page says 37 and under, AMH 6 to 35 and BMI below 35. The PURE page says under 38, AMH above 10, BMI below 30 and regular periods. Someone with an AMH of 8 and a BMI of 32 is eligible on one page and not on the other, and finds out which at a £220 consultation. It costs an hour of an editor’s time to fix, once someone decides which is right.
