Quick wins

22 smaller changes to cambridge-ivf.nhs.uk, each shown on its own against the site as it is today. Any of them can be made independently of the five big improvements and of each other, and most need nothing more than an editor in the content management system.

Open one, then use Today and Proposed to compare. Switch Notes on to see why we suggest it.

Costs and packages

  • QW1

    Fix the conflicting PURE criteria

    Agree one set of PURE criteria with the clinical lead and make the prices page and the PURE page say the same thing.

    Try: switch to Proposed.
  • QW2

    Show the HFEA rating next to every add-on

    Show the HFEA’s traffic-light rating beside every optional add-on the clinic sells, with a line on why it might be suggested.

    Try: switch to Proposed.
  • QW3

    Lead with staged payments on package pages

    Put “pay for cycle 1 now, later cycles only if you need them” at the top of the Duo and Multi-Cycle pages, with the amounts.

    Try: switch to Proposed.
  • QW4

    Tidy the prices page

    Correct the typos on the prices page, show the add-ons once in their own table and check the gamete transport fees.

    Try: switch to Proposed.
