What we're proposing

Why

The prices page is the one people read most carefully, and it has “chalmydia”, “IICSI”, “Human and Fertilisation (HFEA)” and “pregnancy scan.pregnancy scan.” The add-on list is repeated under nearly every package. Transporting gametes overseas costs £330, or £300 with a special export directive, which may be right but looks like a mistake. Small things, but on a page asking for several thousand pounds they make people wonder what else is wrong.
