What we're proposing

Write unique titles and descriptions for the top 20 pages, and add short FAQs to the pages people search for.

Why

Several pages share the same description (“Cambridge fertility clinic, 1 hr from London…”), and the search results page on the site has no title at all. Bourn Hall, ten miles away, competes for the same searches: “IVF Cambridge”, “egg freezing Cambridge”, “IVF cost Cambridge”. Clear titles and descriptions that mention price and “NHS” are free, and earn more of those clicks.
