What we're proposing

Show the next information session dates on the homepage and key service pages, with booking on the page.

Why

The free information session is the clinic’s best way in: fifteen minutes each with an embryologist, a nurse and the finance manager. The homepage has a “Book a free Information Session” button but no date, and booking happens on a separate site. Herts & Essex shows its next open evening date on its homepage; Bourn Hall runs an open evening in Cambridge. A date gives people a reason to act this week rather than “sometime”.
