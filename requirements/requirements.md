# Requirements

Each requirement maps to one or more idea numbers in `data/ideas.json`. Quick wins (QW1 to QW22) map to ideas in `data/quick-wins.json`.

## Global
- R01 Prototype navigator replaces the client's header: two tabs (Big improvements, Quick wins), numbered links, Notes switch off by default.
- R02 Landing page with one card per prototype, a "Try:" prompt and How to review. No version, date or authorship line.
- R03 Previous / next pager as the footer, staying within the current tab.
