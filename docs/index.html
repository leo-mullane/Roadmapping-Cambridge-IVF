Five ideas for cambridge-ivf.nhs.uk

Clickable prototypes of five bigger improvements to the Cambridge IVF website, and a set of quick wins that can each be made on their own. Everything that should work does: try the cost estimator, book a call, check your likely package.

Big improvements Quick wins Read the findings report

The big improvements

  1. Clear costs
    1

    Clear costs

    An honest total before the first appointment: an estimator, the packages side by side, the HFEA's rating next to every add-on and the ways to pay.

    Try: pick PURE Duo, say you're not sure about ICSI, then tick the endometrial receptivity test and see what the HFEA says about it.

    Open prototype
  2. Find your path
    2

    Find your path

    A starting point for each kind of patient, a quick check of which package is likely to fit, an NHS funding check and the whole journey on one page.

    Try: choose "Female couples", then enter an age of 36 and a BMI of 27 in the package check.

    Open prototype
  3. Start today
    3

    Start today

    Enquire, book a call with the patient team, book an information session or refer yourself, without leaving the page and at any hour.

    Try: send the short enquiry with an email address missing, then book a call for next week.

    Open prototype
  4. Proof you can trust
    4

    Proof you can trust

    Current success rates by age with the national average alongside, the people who'll look after you, stories from every kind of family and why an NHS clinic is different.

    Try: switch the chart to "per egg collection", then filter the team to embryologists and open a profile.

    Open prototype
  5. Self-pay services
    5

    Self-pay services

    An egg freezing hub with the full cost over time, plus online booking for private semen analysis, a donor pre-screen and storage payments.

    Try: set storage to ten years in the egg freezing calculator, then book a semen analysis in Ipswich.

    Open prototype

How to review

  • Use the bar at the top to move between prototypes, or the previous and next links at the bottom of each page.
  • Switch Notes on to see what each prototype proposes and why, with numbered notes on how things behave. Yellow notes explain; red notes are open questions.
  • Cambridge IVF's own header and footer are left out on purpose, so the changes are what you look at.
  • Prices are the ones published on the live prices page. Success-rate figures by age, names and stories are samples, marked as such.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. Forms show what would happen without submitting.
