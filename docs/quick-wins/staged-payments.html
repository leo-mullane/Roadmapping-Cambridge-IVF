Quick win 3 of 22 · Costs and packages

What we're proposing

Put “pay for cycle 1 now, later cycles only if you need them” at the top of the Duo and Multi-Cycle pages, with the amounts.

Why

Staged payment is a real advantage: Multi-Cycle is £11,540 in total but £8,390 to start, and nobody pays for cycles they don’t use. Today it’s mentioned in one sentence at the top of the prices page and not at all at the top of the package pages. Leading with it makes the higher-value packages feel less daunting, at no cost beyond an editor’s afternoon.
