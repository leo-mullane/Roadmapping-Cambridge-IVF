After a positive test

  1. Early pregnancy scan at Cambridge IVF, included in your package.
  2. Handover to the Rosie, part of the same hospital trust, with your notes.
  3. Specialist care if you need it: high-risk obstetrics, fetal medicine and one of the UK’s largest neonatal units.
