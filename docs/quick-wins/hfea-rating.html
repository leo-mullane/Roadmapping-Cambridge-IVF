Quick win 10 of 22 · Trust

What we're proposing

Ask every patient at their review appointment to rate the clinic on the HFEA site, with a card, a QR code and a follow-up email.

Why

The HFEA profile shows a 5 out of 5 inspection rating and a 2 out of 5 patient rating from only 22 people, and it appears on every clinic search and comparison on the HFEA site. With so few ratings, a few dozen more from ordinary, satisfied patients would move the score. This may be the cheapest revenue lever on the whole list: a printed card and a line in an email.
