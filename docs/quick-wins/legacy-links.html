Quick win 15 of 22 · Content clean-up

What we're proposing

Replace links to the old cambridge-ivf.org.uk domain and the Outlook “safelinks” on the feedback page with direct links.

Why

The egg freezing and health professionals pages still link to the old domain. The feedback page’s links to the HFEA, the GMC and the NMC are wrapped in Outlook safelinks that include a member of staff’s email address, presumably pasted from an email. That’s a small privacy slip as well as untidy. Fixing it, and adding a broken-link check to a monthly routine, is an hour’s work.
