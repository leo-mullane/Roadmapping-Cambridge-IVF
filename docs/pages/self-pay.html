Prototype 5 of 5 · Self-pay services

What we're proposing

Three services people pay for directly, moved online: booking and paying for a private semen analysis with the current wait shown at the point of booking, a short eligibility pre-screen for would-be sperm donors, and a page to pay storage fees with a reference number.

Why

The andrology service runs more than 500 semen analyses a month across three labs, with waits of 26 to 34 working days, and private tests are booked by phone on a dedicated line. The sperm donor questionnaire is a PDF to print and post, at a time when donor sperm is in short supply and each donor supports several treatment cycles. Storage at £346 a year is recurring income, and there's no way to pay it online that we could see. Each of these swaps a phone call or a letter for something that works at 10pm, and Guy's and St Thomas' already takes payments through GOV.UK Pay.
