Prototype 2 of 5 · Find your path: your journey

What we're proposing

Publish the "Your journey" page the menu already promises: one timeline from first enquiry to the early pregnancy scan and the Rosie. Each step says roughly how long it takes, what it costs and who you'll see, with a switch between the NHS-funded and self-funded routes.

Why

Today the "Your journey" link returns "Page not found", and the steps are spread across the lab page, the support information page and seven package pages. People about to spend several thousand pounds want to know what they're signing up to and how long it will take. Joined-up care through to the Rosie is also one of the clearest things an NHS clinic can offer that a private one can't, and at the moment it's on a page that leads with a royal visit in 2013.

Your journey

What happens, step by step

Every journey is different, but most follow these steps. Open any step for what happens, how long it takes and what it costs.

  1. Enquire online, book a call with the patient team or come to a free information session, where you can speak to an embryologist, a fertility nurse and our finance manager.

    Your GP or hospital consultant refers you. We'll write to you with an appointment once we've checked your area's funding criteria.

    CostFreeWho you'll seePatient team
    Start today

  2. Blood tests including AMH, a 3D ultrasound scan and, for couples, a semen analysis. You'll have your results before your consultation.

    Cost£375 to £595CostNHS-fundedWhereCambridge or Ipswich

  3. A consultant goes through your results and recommends a treatment and package. Ask anything. You'll leave with a written plan and a costed quote.

    CostIncluded in the assessment, or £220CostNHS-fundedWho you'll seeFertility consultant

    Behaviour"A written plan and a costed quote" is a proposed change to how the clinic works, not just the site. It's the fix for the HFEA "way above the estimate" ratings.

  4. Screening tests, consent forms and two counselling sessions. If you're using a donor, this is when you choose one, with implications counselling to help you think it through.

    CostIncluded in your packageWho you'll seeNurse and counsellor

  5. Medication to stimulate your ovaries, monitoring scans, then egg collection under sedation at Kefford House. In the lab, eggs are fertilised and embryos grow in the EmbryoScope for five or six days.

    CostYour packageCostNHS-fundedWhereScans in Cambridge or Ipswich, egg collection in Cambridge
    See package prices

  6. A short procedure to place one embryo in the womb. Any good-quality embryos left over can be frozen for later.

    Freezing£765 including the first yearStorage after that£346 a year

  7. Whatever the result, you'll have a review appointment or an early pregnancy scan, and counselling is there if you need it, usually within two weeks.

  8. Because we're part of Cambridge University Hospitals, your care can continue at the Rosie maternity hospital, including specialist services if you need them.
