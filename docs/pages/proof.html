Prototype 4 of 5 · Proof you can trust

What we're proposing

A results and people page that does justice to the clinic: the latest HFEA figures with the national average alongside, success by age in both of the HFEA's measures, the team who'll look after you, stories from every kind of family, why an NHS clinic is different, and a simple routine for asking patients to rate the clinic on the HFEA site.

Why

The success rates page uses data from 2019 and 2020, shown as pictures of charts, and the newest patient story mentions 2013. There are no staff profiles. Meanwhile the HFEA, which is where many self-funders shortlist clinics, shows Cambridge IVF with a 5 out of 5 inspection rating and a 2 out of 5 patient rating from only 22 people. Five-year-old numbers read as something to hide, which is a shame, because the current ones are perfectly respectable: 31% of embryos transferred led to a birth in 2024, which the HFEA calls consistent with the national 35%. A few dozen more patient ratings would also move that 2 out of 5.

Reading these numbers

"Per egg collection" counts every transfer from one round of egg collection, fresh and frozen, so it's closer to what one cycle of treatment gives you. Small differences between clinics usually come down to the patients they treat, as the HFEA says. Your consultant can talk about your own chances.
